# Notifications

gbans surfaces notifications to players in two ways: an in-app notification center, and —
for match results — an in-game private message.

## In-app notification center

The notification bell in the top bar shows your unread count and links to
`/notifications`. From there you can:

- Browse your notifications, most recent first, with their read state, severity
  (info / warning / error), message, author, and an optional link to the related page.
- Filter and sort the list.
- Open a notification's link to jump straight to the relevant page.

If your browser allows it and you've enabled it from **Settings → General**, new
notifications are also raised as silent desktop alerts.

## Match notifications

When a match you took part in is imported, gbans can send you an in-game private message
linking to your match page. This is controlled by the
**Notify me in-game when my match results are ready** option in **Settings → General**.

- Only players who have opted in receive the message.
- The message is only sent while the player is still connected to one of the tracked
  servers.
- Each player is notified once per match, no matter how many times they appear in it.
